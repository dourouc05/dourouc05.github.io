# Teaching material {#teaching-material}

## Discrete optimisation

Due to my teaching-assistant role, I have developed an exercise book for the discrete optimisation course. It also contains a set of final answers and worked-out solutions, so that the book may readily be used in different contexts. It is [freely available on GitHub](https://github.com/dourouc05/OptimisationTeachingKit) (under a CC-NC 4 license).

## Robotics

As a teaching assistant, I had the opportunity to contribute to Renaud Detry's robotics courseware, [teaching robotics with a simulator](https://github.com/ULgRobotics/trs). My role has been to ease the first steps with MATLAB and the simulator, by splitting the main demonstration script into smaller units (work done with Mathieu Baijot) and by creating videos showing the use of the simulator V-REP:
